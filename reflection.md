# Final Reflection — The Digital Translator

A computer does not understand the real world the way humans do. It needs information to be represented in a format it can process and clear instructions to tell it what to do.

For example, when someone asks a computer to make breakfast, it needs specific information about the food, ingredients, quantities and preparation steps.

Computers also use algorithms to solve problems, process data and produce results.

From this mission, I learned that computers depend on instructions and data to perform tasks correctly. I also understood the importance of input, processing, output, data representation and error handling.

**My biggest lesson:** Before writing a program, I need to understand the problem, identify the information required and plan the steps the computer must follow.
