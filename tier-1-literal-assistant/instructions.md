# Tier 1 - My solution

## 1. Required Ingredients
- Water
- Milk
- Chocolate
- Sugar

## 2. Required Equipment

- Cup
- Spoon
- Pot

## 3. Preparation Steps

- Set up the cooking fire.
- Place the pot on the fire.
- Add water and allow it to boil.
- Add two spoons of milk.
- Add one and a half spoons of chocolate.
- Add two cubes of sugar.
- Stir until properly mixed.
- Serve in a cup.

## 4. completion codition
- The drink is properly mixed and ready to serve.

## Reasoning Challenge
When the original instruction only said "Make me breakfast," I had to make several assumptions that a computer could not make on its own:
1. **Menu choice:** I assumed the user wanted a hot chocolate drink rather than another breakfast meal.
2. **Measurements:** I assumed the exact quantities needed (e.g., two spoons of milk, two cubes of sugar).
3. **Tools and method:** I assumed the ingredients should be boiled in a pot over a fire and served in a cup.
